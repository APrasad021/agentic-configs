# ADR 0000: <the decision, as a statement>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-00XX](./00XX-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true that forces a decision. Constraints, deadlines, what already
exists. No opinions yet — someone who disagrees with the outcome should still
recognise this section as accurate.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. "We will …"

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard. **The second half is the point.**
An ADR that lists only benefits is marketing, and it will not help the person
who finds it in two years wondering why the code looks like this.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and the specific reason it lost. Usually the most
valuable section, because the alternative that lost is exactly what someone
will propose again.
